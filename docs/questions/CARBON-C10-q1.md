# Question CARBON-C10-q1: Zoom ramp policy

**Ticket:** C10

**Source:** Lapse §1

**Status:** Answered for Carbon by principal-approved product decision

**Status reviewed:** 2026-11-03

## Question

How should framing taps transition and interact with the shutter?

## Approved answer and explanation

Start testing roughly 200 ms without overshoot. Do not delay capture for decorative animation; ensure preview and capture framing agree. Measure hardware transitions; the duration is a starting target, not a recovered Lapse rate.

Authority: [D-025](../decisions/D-025-amateur-camera-defaults.md#c10-zoom-transitions), approved by the principal to serve a novice photographing people with zero setup.

This answers Carbon's product behavior without establishing unknown reference-app behavior. It does not mark the feature implemented or device-verified. See D-025 for the rationale and remaining validation.
