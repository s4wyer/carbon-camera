# Question CARBON-R05-q1: Grain render order

**Ticket:** R05

**Source:** No Fusion §3; Halide §2; Lapse §6 and §7

**Status:** Answered for Carbon by principal-approved product decision

**Status reviewed:** 2026-11-03

## Question

Where should grain run when reference call-site order is unknown?

## Approved answer and explanation

After color and before text/borders and encoding, with a saved seed and consistent apparent size. Natural has zero added grain; creative presets may enable it. Verify preview/export consistency.

Authority: [D-025](../decisions/D-025-amateur-camera-defaults.md#r05-grain-order-and-default), approved by the principal to serve a novice photographing people with zero setup.

This answers Carbon's product behavior without establishing unknown reference-app behavior. It does not mark the feature implemented or device-verified. See D-025 for the rationale and remaining validation.
