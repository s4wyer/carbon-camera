## Question

What film controls should Carbon expose without recovered reference slider bounds?

## Approved answer and explanation

One Strength control from 0–100%; zero removes creative treatment while keeping basic correction, and 100 applies the authored look. Optional grain/glow/vignette maximums require visual testing.

Authority: [D-025](../decisions/D-025-amateur-camera-defaults.md#r07-film-controls), approved by the principal to serve a novice photographing people with zero setup.

This answers Carbon's product behavior without establishing unknown reference-app behavior. It does not mark the feature implemented or device-verified. See D-025 for the rationale and remaining validation.
