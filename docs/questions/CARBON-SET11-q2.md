# Question CARBON-SET11-q2: Default preset and format

**Ticket:** SET11

**Source:** No Fusion §9; D-002; D-006

**Status:** Answered for Carbon by principal-approved product decision

**Status reviewed:** 2026-11-03

## Question

What should Carbon select on first launch?

## Approved answer and explanation

Natural, auto exposure and white balance, ordinary HEIF output, RAW retained where supported by the active combination, and a supported processed fallback with provenance otherwise. Offer JPEG compatibility export. Validate combinations and the primary/fallback pipeline.

Authority: [D-025](../decisions/D-025-amateur-camera-defaults.md#set11-q2-launch-preset-and-format), approved by the principal to serve a novice photographing people with zero setup.

This answers Carbon's product behavior without establishing unknown reference-app behavior. It does not mark the feature implemented or device-verified. See D-025 for the rationale and remaining validation.
